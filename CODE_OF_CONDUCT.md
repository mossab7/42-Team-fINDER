# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, doxxing, or publication of private student information.

Examples of positive behavior include giving actionable feedback, accepting correction, respecting different experience levels, and keeping technical discussions focused on the project.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove issues, comments, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

For platform abuse or urgent safety concerns, use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools. Security vulnerabilities must be reported privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`.
